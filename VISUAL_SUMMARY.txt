💡 PRO TIPS

1. Work in phases - don't do everything at once
2. Test each phase before moving to the next
3. Use TAILWIND_CLASS_MAPPING.md as your reference
4. Keep dark mode classes intact - add light mode alongside
5. Use browser DevTools to verify colors
6. Check contrast ratios with a tool (WebAIM, WAVE)
7. Test on real mobile devices, not just browser zoom
8. Consider color blindness - test with simulator
9. Screenshot dark and light side-by-side
10. Get feedback before final deployment
